When patients present to physicians with an elevated risk of coronary artery disease, or CAD, cardiologists have many tests at their disposal. Increasingly, cardiologists are turning to cardiac computed tomography angiogram (CCTA) as an accurate, non-invasive tool for diagnosing and guiding their treatment strategy for CAD.
