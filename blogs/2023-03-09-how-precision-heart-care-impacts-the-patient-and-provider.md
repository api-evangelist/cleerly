According to the American Heart Association (AHA), nearly half of Americans have some type of cardiovascular disease. 1 Despite such wide prevalence, we struggle to provide these patients with the care they need. When patients present to their physicians with high cholesterol, high blood pressure, and other conditions traditionally associated with heart disease, the current standard of care is to refer them for tests or clinical services that don’t actually measure their true heart disease risk.
